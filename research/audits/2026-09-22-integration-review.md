## Current research and what was promoted

The [primary-source research report](../decision-model-review-2026-09-21.md)
contains 15 source groups with versions, dates, contracts versus reported
results, limitations, and 13 explicitly unrun experiments. The review also
checked [GEPA's current paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457) before
refining prompt-optimization guidance.

Promoted into the appropriate reference, once:

- Current Jev primitive semantics and jaggedness reinforce exact-work and
  question-design boundaries. Moving aliases require resolved-model receipts.
- Proper scores, tied-bin reliability, selective risk/coverage, and explicit
  review costs replace ECE-only reasoning. All-abstain risk is undefined,
  not zero; an impossible observed probability has infinite log loss.
- Conformal guarantees are tied to their assumptions, loss, and population;
  marginal coverage is not a per-singleton, subgroup, or permission claim.
- Classical tabular models and tabular foundation models are first-class
  candidates for structured state. Current releases/licenses are not the
  same evidence as an older paper's evaluated checkpoint.
- Prediction, decision-focused optimization, and causal policy learning
  answer different questions. A lateness predictor cannot establish that
  fining a supplier will improve delivery.
- Cascades must account for route-conditioned calibration, the harder
  fallback population, total overhead, and simple always-small/always-large
  baselines. Stateless judgment does not imply zero provider retention or
  measured low latency.
- Reflective prompt optimization needs a budget, independent development
  feedback, and untouched final evaluation; reported GEPA gains are not a
  transferable quality guarantee.

Recent selective/action-conditional conformal preprints remain research-watch
items, not a recommended default or a claimed implemented capability.
No paid model bake-off, hosted decision inference, causal experiment, or
benchmark reproduction was run. The report makes those gaps explicit.
