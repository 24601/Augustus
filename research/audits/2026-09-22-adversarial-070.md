## Verified findings against 9b4d86d

### P2: finite means overflow or silently change

[`compare_workflows.py:59`](https://github.com/24601/Augustus/blob/9b4d86d96c92656a97af521c9ed5a5ccad905034/.agents/skills/augustus/scripts/compare_workflows.py#L59)
divides every value by n before `math.fsum`. Over real numbers this is
equivalent; in floating-point arithmetic it is not stable:

| Input to `_mean` | Observed | Correct representable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[sys.float_info.max] * 3` | `OverflowError: intermediate overflow in fsum` | `sys.float_info.max` |
| `[5e-324] * 2` | `0.0` | `5e-324` |
| `[1e-323] * 3` | `1.5e-323` | `1e-323` |

Complete valid receipts reproduce the defect. Loss, cost and latency share this
path. One subnormal receipt reports zero incumbent mean loss but a negative
candidate-minus-incumbent delta. The old `1e308` test misses float maximum.

### P2: mixed selective cost overflows although all case costs are finite

[`evaluate_decisions.py:325`](https://github.com/24601/Augustus/blob/9b4d86d96c92656a97af521c9ed5a5ccad905034/.agents/skills/augustus/scripts/evaluate_decisions.py#L325)
adds separately rounded weighted costs. Use `(p,y)` rows:

```python
[(1, 0), (0, 1), (0, 1), (.5, 0), (.5, 1)]
```

At thresholds `.2/.8`, one FP, two FN and two abstentions each incur the same
cost `sys.float_info.max`. The mean must equal that finite cost, but the original
returns infinity. A parent count-partition probe through n=120 found 8,596
overflowing three-category partitions at this cost. These are arithmetic
fixtures, not observed product failures.

### P3: negative-class log loss cancels small probabilities

[`evaluate_decisions.py:140`](https://github.com/24601/Augustus/blob/9b4d86d96c92656a97af521c9ed5a5ccad905034/.agents/skills/augustus/scripts/evaluate_decisions.py#L140)
forms `1-p` before taking its logarithm. At `p=1e-20, y=0`, it returns zero;
the stable result is `-math.log1p(-1e-20) == 1e-20`. The defect also occurs at
`5e-324` and `1e-17`. It predates the new comparator; the release review still
treats it as worth correcting.
