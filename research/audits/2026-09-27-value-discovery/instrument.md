agent answers built two application briefs and a private outcome grader. The
coordinator verified archive hashes and reran the full validation in the Linux

## What the tasks require

Cold-chain sleeve dispatch discloses that logger temperature and laboratory
disposition arrive after delivery, and that carriers changed equipment on day 201.
Printshop preflight routing supplies a shuffled multi-revision event export whose
reprint billing retries more often than ordinary invoices. Both state the
objective, action costs, serving keys, resource limits and the owner's disclosed
deployment rule. Neither names a model, split, primitive or the hazard itself.
Agents write `fit`, a separate-process `predict`, and a `report.json`.

Facts are disclosed; the resolution is not. That is the intended difference from
the saturated earlier tasks, where prompts supplied the decisive choices.

## Coordinator replay, Linux orb, Python 3.11

```sh
python3 private/validate.py public --output /tmp/pilot-replay.json
python3 private/grade.py public coldchain private/minimal.py --seeds 77001 77002 77003
```

Validation reproduced the author's forty runs exactly: independent hand-derived
anchors pass; minimal and careful references reach $2.210 and $1.766 mean future
dollars; careless automatic feature search reaches $5.733 and $2.400; stale-period,
delivery-only-feature, retry-weighted and superseded-label competitors lose money
against both references. Five report mutations keep identical policy loss and each
fail recomputation. Seven serving mutations fail serving, including a stateful
policy whose descriptive report is correct, so the two flags are independent.
On unseen seeds 77001–77003 the minimal rule scored $1.9417 against the $3.00
incumbent, so discrimination is not specific to the author's seeds.

The grader reconstructs population, counts, errors, loss and the deployment
recommendation from source records read **before** candidate execution, reruns
the artifact on serving-only projections, restarts prediction in reverse order
and as a singleton, enforces artifact size, memory, CPU and wall limits, and
rejects working-directory mutation. It labels historical split independence
`unverifiable` rather than trusting agent-supplied ID lists.

## Limits carried forward

Data and intervention effects are synthetic and perfect; no causal effect was
estimated from observation. Pilot seeds are development evidence. The harness
separates processes but is not an adversarial sandbox, so candidate authoring
environments must contain public inputs only. A minimal no-ceremony rule already
performs as well as the careful reference: the instrument can detect costly
mistakes, but it cannot reward method, and agents may simply not make those
mistakes. Neither agent uplift nor the 40-turn/600-second budget is piloted yet.
Publishing these two archives consumes them as future unseen evaluations.
