## The three contrasts

Utilities are higher-is-better, so superiority reads on the **lower** bound: the left arm wins when
LCB > +0.02.

| Contrast | Powered | Search mean | Confirmation | Interval | Reading |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| best explicit − implicit | yes | +0.0967 | **+0.0945** | [+0.0741, +0.1148] | **superior** |
| best explicit − best constant | no | +0.0134 | **−0.0044** | [−0.0171, +0.0084] | not superior; sign flipped |
| (vii) outcome − (vi) proxy | yes | +0.0600 | **+0.0375** | [+0.0163, +0.0588] | **powered and does not clear the margin** |

### Explicit control beats endogenous control, decisively

The reader asked to choose for itself abstains on **50.4%** of questions and reaches 0.119 exact
match; the thresholded policy abstains never and reaches 0.247. That is the whole +0.094. This is
the clearest thing E3 says: a model given the option to stop, expand or abstain, at the same
evidence and with the same weights, gives up on half the questions and does worse.

### Reading everything is as good as choosing when to stop

The explicit arm stops after 2.58 rounds on average and scores 0.1609. Always reading all six
paragraphs costs a full round more and scores 0.1653. On search the explicit arm was ahead by
+0.0134; on confirmation it is **behind by 0.0044**. The interval lies inside ±margin, so the
mechanical equivalence flag is true, and the analysis lock had already declared the equivalence
readings inconclusive before confirmation was read, so **no equivalence conclusion is drawn**. The
scorer prints the flag and the lock governs what may be concluded; the runner flagged that
distinction rather than smoothing it, which is the right handling.

At λ = 0.1 the most a policy can save by stopping early is 0.067 of utility, and the accuracy it
gives up by reading less is about the same size. The honest reading is that at this λ, on this
task, with this reader, the round budget is not where the value is.

### P5 is powered, and it fails

This is the row to think about. On search, outcome-selected beat proxy-selected by +0.060, and the
lock computed that 3,072 questions would suffice at that effect. Confirmation had 6,405 — twice
what was required — and the realized effect is **+0.0375 with LCB +0.0163, below the +0.02 margin**.

The contrast is powered and does not adopt. That is not a contradiction: **power was computed at
the search-split effect, and the search-split effect was optimistic because the arm was selected on
that split.** The analysis lock said this in advance, in as many words: "The planning effect is the
search-split mean, not an independently registered number. A search mean is an estimate, and an
optimistic one for the arm that was selected on that split." Here it cost 37% of the effect, and
that was enough to move a claim from supported to not supported.

The direction is right and the size is not. Selecting a stopping threshold on a label-free proxy
does cost utility — the proxy arm stops after 1.007 rounds and reaches 0.157 exact match against
0.247 — but 0.0375 with this σ̂ is not a 0.02-margin win.

## P6 is not supported

| | Rate |
| --- | ---: |
| False adoption, adopt-best | 0.005 |
| False adoption, incumbent–challenger | **0.000** |
| Difference | −0.005, 95% CI [−0.0148, +0.0048] |

The direction matches P6 and the interval crosses zero, so the claim is **not supported**. With 200
resplits of 500 and an incumbent that is genuinely near-best, adopt-best was wrong 1 time in 200;
there is not enough false adoption to measure a reduction in. That is a statement about this
setting, not about the mechanism: E4a measured the same machinery where defects were planted and it
behaved as required.

## Both controls pass, and one of them needed fixing first

- **Identical frozen incumbent against its own copy**: max |Δ| exactly 0, adoptions 0.
- **Planted +0.05 shift**: adopted 200 of 200, at a computed sample size of **615**.

The planted control initially never adopted, and the reason was not timidity: at R = 2.4 and m = 3
the empirical Bernstein range term alone is 0.0615 at n = 500, so no effect of 0.05 is detectable
there at all. A control reporting a real effect going unadopted, when the interval cannot see it,
would have looked like a finding. It now runs at the smallest size its own requirement implies and
reports that size.

## The determinism failure travels with all of this

The pre-registered rerun check failed at **100% joint per-question disagreement** against a 5%
tolerance ([receipt](e3-determinism-2026-09-24.md)). The consequence was applied without amendment:
the replay tables are the sole source, every arm and resplit reads one frozen table, and the rate is
recorded in the lock, in the scores file and here. Arms that threshold `p_answerable` carry a rerun
instability bounded at up to 2.4× the margin, which limits transportability to a regenerated table
and not the internal comparison.

## Limits

- One task, one reader on confirmation, one λ. The 4B reader contributed to search only.
- HotpotQA is public and the readers are pretrained, so the superpopulation reading carries the
  contamination limit explicitly. The finite-population Δ does not.
- P6's interval is over resplits of one finite population, not over draws from a superpopulation.
- The equivalence readings were declared inconclusive before confirmation and remain so. Intervals
  are printed because an unpowered contrast is reported with its interval and counts toward no
  outcome row in either direction.
- The split's analysis-lock binding was recorded after the split was staged and after the replay
  began, and before the scoring run. The replay reads inputs only: the staged confirmation rows
  carry `id`, `question` and `paragraphs` and nothing else, 0 of 6,405 carry an answer field, the
  replay never mounted `/srv/aug/ctl`, and `augexp` cannot read the answers file, which is mode 600
  under `augctl`. That the scoring run was the **first** read of a confirmation answer is a
  custodian assertion about an unlogged interval, not a kernel trace: the box runs no auditd and
  `/srv` is `noatime`, so an earlier open cannot be disproved, only made inaccessible by custody.
  Ordering rests on the public commit, not on the binding.
- "Published" for the split means the local staging write at 2026-09-24T20:33:43Z. The split
  manifest is not in the public tree; only the partitioner is. The manifest predates the lock file
  and the lock commit, and the preserved unbound copy has no `analysis_lock` slot at all.

## The custody guard was tested, and it refused

Corroborated 2026-09-26 by the original execution thread, from inputs and receipts only, with no
rerun and no labels reopened.

The scorer was handed a deliberately wrong analysis-lock hash before any answer file was opened. It
exited 2 with:

    error: analysis-lock mismatch: supplied 0000000000000000…, recorded fcb626f07d0884f6…;
    refusing to open the answers

The audit shows **zero open events** for the confirmation answers on that attempt, and the answer
file's atime and mtime were unchanged. **The guard is not a convention; it is a check that runs
before the labels are reachable**, and it has now been exercised deliberately in E3 and relied on
in E1's replication, where predictions bound to a different lock could not have been scored against
the recorded one.

## NVIDIA replication, 2026-09-26: every conclusion holds and the arms do not move

Scored with the frozen `e3_score.py` (`dfaf7f02…`, unchanged) on an NVIDIA confirmation replay table
and the shipped search sigma. The original ROCm report was not overwritten; both sit beside each
other.

| contrast | ROCm mean / LCB | NVIDIA mean / LCB | claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (vii) outcome − (vi) proxy | +0.037533 / +0.016284 | +0.035977 / +0.014665 | **P5 powered and failed on both**, margin 0.02 |
| best explicit − implicit | +0.094457 / +0.074101 | +0.092110 / +0.071811 | explicit beats implicit, both |
| best explicit − best constant | −0.004372 / −0.017120 | −0.003908 / −0.016679 | explicit does **not** beat reading everything, both |

Arm means, ROCm then NVIDIA: explicit 0.160890 / 0.159480, implicit 0.066432 / 0.067369, constant
0.165262 / 0.163388, proxy 0.123357 / 0.123502. **Every number agrees to the third decimal and
every verdict is identical.** The plan named P5 as the uncertain row in advance; it failed by the
same margin on both machines, so its failure is not a hardware artifact.

**Arm selection is identical.** Re-running `e3_sigma.choose` and `contrasts_for` unchanged on the
fresh NVIDIA search table selects `composite @ 0.8` for both `best_explicit` and `outcome_selected`,
`k=6` for `best_constant`, and `composite @ 0.05` for `proxy_selected` — the same four choices the
ROCm table produced. Search mean utilities move in the third decimal. The confirmation decisions did
not need rebuilding.

That matters more than the scores. A table whose every row differs bitwise across vendors, and whose
per-question joint identity is 0.0 by construction, still selects the same arms and reproduces the
same verdicts. **The bitwise instability recorded in the determinism receipt does not propagate to
any conclusion this experiment draws.**

### Three limits, all reported rather than smoothed over

- `e3_sigma.main()` was **not** called: it refuses unless it sees two readers and exactly six
  contrasts, and the 4B NVIDIA search table was never shipped. The functions were used directly and
  unedited. A full `main()` replication needs that second table.
- The NVIDIA stack is vLLM 0.30.0, torch 2.13.0+cu130, transformers 5.16.1, which are not
  tabputer's. **A cross-vendor difference cannot be attributed to the vendor.** The same-box rerun
  holds that stack fixed and is unaffected.
- `e3-nvidia-rerun-determinism-search.json` carries a `comparison` field copied from the
  cross-platform template, describing a ROCm-against-NVIDIA comparison. Its hashes and its 0.0000
  disagreement are the **second NVIDIA replay against the first**. The file is mislabelled; the
  measurement is not.

The frozen scorer's own limits sentence still says the determinism check failed at 100%. That
sentence is scoped to the ROCm receipt it was written from, and the script was not edited to soften
it — a frozen artifact that describes the machine it was frozen on is behaving correctly, and the
narrowing belongs in the receipts rather than in the code.
