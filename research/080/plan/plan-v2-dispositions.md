# Review dispositions for Augustus 0.8.0 plan v2

**Confidential and local only.**

- **Reviews:** `reviews/fable-5.1-xhigh.md` (Fable), and the final `codex` block of
  `reviews/astra-max.raw.log` (Astra). Plan v2 records requested and observed identities.
- **Plan:** `plan-v2.md`. Section numbers below refer to it.
- **Coverage:** all 38 findings. That is Fable P0-1, P1-1 to P1-10 and P2-1 to P2-11, and Astra
  1 to 16 (Astra 1–13 are P1; 14–16 are P2).
- **Disposition values:**
  - **Accepted:** the correction is adopted as the reviewer proposed it.
  - **Modified:** the defect is accepted, but the fix differs.
  - **Rejected:** the defect is not accepted.
- **Result:** no finding was rejected. Where both reviewers raised the same defect, one fix covers
  both, and the rows point to each other.
- **Arithmetic:** the reviewers' numbers were re-derived locally with `calc_v2.py` in the
  scratchpad, and all of them reproduce. Examples:
  - 64.2%, 26.4% and 7.55% A/A tails;
  - a 13.9% zero-in-20 bound;
  - the sign-test case: +0.0292 mean loss at p = 0.0068;
  - the sampling case: −0.667 apparent against +0.9 true;
  - Hoeffding n = 2,397 and 3,684.
