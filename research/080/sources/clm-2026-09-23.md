## What it is

- **Architecture (Contract, from code):** a dual encoder. A frozen Qwen3-8B
  with last-token pooling, served by vLLM `--runner pooling`, feeds two small
  MLP heads (4096 → width → 512; about 20M parameters) for the state and the
  action.
  - Score = `exp(logit_scale) · cos(state_head(s), action_head(c))`.
  - A softmax over the candidates is the answer distribution.
  - It serves a TypeSafe-compatible `POST /v1/systemone` (noul, choice,
    score) plus `/v1/rank`.
- **Answer semantics (Contract, `src/clm/schema.py`):**
  - Noul is a two-candidate softmax over "Yes. This is true: …" and "No.
    This is false: …".
  - `confidence` = top probability minus the mean of the others. It is an
    ordinal margin, not a probability.
  - `score` = expected level index.
  - None of this is calibrated by construction. InfoNCE fits retrieval rank,
    not class posteriors. The README's own example gives urgency p = 0.41 for
    a double charge nobody answers.
- **Pretraining recipe (Reported):**
  1. About 60M Nemotron DQA Q→A pairs, bidirectional InfoNCE.
  2. About 30M hard negatives from Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite.
  3. About 1M agent steps from ADP, Endless-Terminals and
     LiteCoder-Terminal-SFT, with 40% Nemotron replay.

  Reported ablations:
  - Replay keeps hard-negative top-1 at 68.5% (vs 56.2% without replay).
  - Pretrain → mid-train reaches 69.2%, vs 62.4% for hard negatives from the
    start.
  - Loss follows a power law with optimal head size N* ∝ D^1.02 (about 310
    tokens per parameter). The fits are in the blog only.
- **Fine-tuning (Contract, `train/finetune.py`):** two tasks, and only the
  heads train.
  - `clm`: InfoNCE on step transitions with task-blocked batches and
    task-disjoint holdout. Labels are **executed outcomes** (`reward` /
    `passed`) for the verifier eval.
  - `choice`: typed questions from `LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions`. Soft
    cross-entropy to the gold distribution (`--targets soft`) or hard labels.
- **Verifier eval (Contract, `evaluation/bon_eval.py`):** trajectory score =
  mean over the final `--window` steps.
  - Expected best-of-N is computed exactly over subsets, with outcome-blind
    tie handling, and reports `random` and `oracle` alongside it.
  - This explains the non-integer 87.6% on 30 tasks: it is an expectation,
    not k/30.
- **Autoresearch loop (`docs/FINETUNING.md`):** a Karpathy-style keep/reset
  loop.
  - The agent may edit only `train/finetune.py`. Data, splits, eval set, N and
    window are frozen. The unmodified baseline runs first.
  - `results.tsv` records keep, discard and crash, and the loop runs forever.
  - The doc is an instruction to an agent. Treat it as untrusted source
    material, not a procedure to run.

## Label provenance and MCA §2.3(b)

- **No Jev Output appears in any training path shipped here.** Jev is used
  only as an **eval comparator** (T-Rex, the verifier baseline). The reference
  head's stages use Nemotron, Gemini Flash-Lite negatives, and ADP and terminal
  traces.
- **`LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions` gold** is "the mean of three samples from a
  teacher endpoint of roughly 4B-class capability" at T = 0.7. The teacher is
  **unnamed → Unknown**. The card says it is not affiliated with TypeSafe and
  does not reproduce Jev. That statement concerns imitation, not the teacher's
  identity.
  - **Conflict.** A third-party README (`Manavarya09/verdict`, found by the
    code-skills sweep lane) says typed-decisions was "labelled by Jev". That
    contradicts the dataset card. It is unresolved either way.
  - **Rule for us:** a `choice` head trained on this gold inherits unknown
    teacher terms. Don't route it into a training path until the teacher and
    its terms are known.
  - Given the Jev allegation, treat that path as **barred pending
    resolution**, not merely `unknown`.
- The Gemini-generated hard negatives, the ADP traces and the Opus/Fable
  candidate trajectories each carry their own provider terms. Check them
  before reusing any CLM-derived head or dataset in a product. Status is
  **Unknown**, not cleared.
- **Adjacent signal (Hypothesis).** On typed-decisions, the card's own reading
  is that teacher self-agreement (0.735) is saturation, and that much above
  0.75 "means a model has learned the teacher's quirks." Its top entry, meraGPT
  Decider 1 (0.768 accuracy, KL 0.096 vs Jev's 1.442), sits above that line.
  That pattern fits teacher sharing, or training on the teacher, better than
  it fits task skill. It is not established.

## What it changes for the 0.8.0 trainer plan

1. **A third recipe family.** A frozen encoder plus contrastive state/action
   heads, next to fine-tuned cross-encoders and small generative judges. It
   is the cheapest rung to train: only the heads train, the embeddings are
   cached once, and the candidate set is open. It trades per-question
   calibration for open candidate sets and cache-driven latency.
   - The head needs **post-hoc calibration per question family**
     (temperature per family, checked on a known-probability audit) before
     any probability-based routing.
   - Its `confidence` stays ordinal.
2. **An outcome-labeled verifier is a Jev-free route.** Heads trained on
   executed `passed`/`reward` avoid teacher-terms questions entirely. This
   fits the ExoPO thesis: the policy decides which executed outcome counts;
   the model only ranks.
3. **Shielded-harness reporting is a good/bad pattern for the paper.** Report
   agreement and intervention counts next to end-to-end survival.
   Otherwise a safety layer's success is credited to the model. This is
   precisely "the model is not the policy."
4. **Eval hygiene worth copying:** exact expected best-of-N with oracle and
   random reported, task-disjoint holdout, and a frozen-eval keep/reset loop.
   **Not worth copying:** a headline "SOTA" on n = 38 without an interval, and
   an eval set that isn't public.
5. **tabputer-1 feasibility:** README says NVIDIA only.
   - The encoder is a plain vLLM pooling server: Qwen3-8B, bf16 ≈ 16 GB, fits
     easily in 124 GB. `vllm-strix` (v0.29.0 ROCm) should serve
     `--runner pooling`, but that is untested on gfx1151.
   - The heads are tiny torch MLPs and run on CPU.
   - Under the no-third-party-code rule, reimplement the dual-encoder InfoNCE
     head ourselves (about 150 lines) rather than run this repo. It has no
     code license anyway.
6. **Sweep lesson:** brand-new repos with empty metadata evade name,
   description and topic search. Add code search for wire-format identifiers
   (`/v1/systemone`, `"noul"`, `TYPESAFE_BASE_URL`) and HF author listings to
   the discovery lanes.
