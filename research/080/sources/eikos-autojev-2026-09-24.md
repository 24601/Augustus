## Why neither is an arm

**Both are Qwen3.8-27B trainings.** AutoJev is full-weight SFT; Eikos-27B is a rank-64 LoRA. Plan
v4 §1 excludes training 9–27B generalists, and the form of the update does not change that.

**AutoJev's training corpus is not published.** The README says "The exact curated training corpus
is not bundled", and the bundled builder produces an earlier 96,000-row corpus, not the curated
73,000-row subset that produced the table. So the no-Jev-in-training claim **cannot be certified
for the model that produced the numbers**, and the rows cannot be inspected. Its own
`results.json` also records the reported panel's role as
`monitored_validation_used_for_checkpoint_selection`, with "Panel membership was amended after
earlier results; not an untouched test", `pretraining_exclusion_known: false` and
`statistical_significance_claim: false`. On the same file's six-suite macro, Jev leads
(0.81842 against 0.81395), and on their 101-item public-hard slice Jev leads 73.27 to 70.30 — so
the headline 84.60% / ECE 0.04282 is a fitted number on the selection panel, not a held-out result.

**Eikos is genuinely not a Jev distillation**, which is worth recording because most of the field
is. Its `eval_jev_suite.py` says in terms: "EVALUATION ONLY (never training: distilling from Jev
is prohibited by its terms)." Its soft targets are GLM-5.3-Flash judgments kept only when the
teacher's argmax matches a writer's unseen gold, programmatic answers computed in code, GSM8K and
TAT-QA gold, and FinEntity human labels. Under §3.6 that resolves to **recorded**, not refused:
the teacher is named with a revision, and decision 12 passes a named hosted teacher through with
provenance recorded.

## What is worth taking

1. **The agreement filter as a pattern.** A writer produces the item and a gold answer; the
   teacher never sees that gold; the row survives only if the teacher's argmax matches. That is a
   cheaper, more honest construction than teacher-as-oracle, and it is close to the double-agreement
   rule already in §3.4.
2. **Decontamination stated as a procedure**, not a promise: 8-gram containment above 0.15 against
   231 public JevBench items, with families, a topic, a language and three trade-rule families held
   out entirely. That is the shape our own overlap audit takes.
3. **Their own honesty about generator-shared suites**: the rules suites "generated by the same
   rule generator family used in training", so transfer is claimed only on unseen trade rules and
   RuleArena. That is exactly the circularity trap M5's design lock names for T1, found
   independently in the wild.

## What must not be imported

Not 82.9, not 2.4%, not 88.3, not 84.60%. Every one is the authors' own harness. Eikos's public
JevBench number is explicitly "public items only, not the official leaderboard run", its own model
card still shows Jev ahead on the general battery (84.1 to 82.5) and on WCB balanced accuracy
(58.6 to 44.5), and RuleArena NBA balanced accuracy is 0.50 for both sizes, which is chance. The
released Eikos data also omits about 2% of the rows the models trained on, so a retrain is "close
to ours but not bit-identical" by the authors' own statement.

## Disposition

- **AutoJev: discovery record only.** Unpublished corpus, a selection panel reported as a result,
  and weights described as private. Not an arm, not a comparator.
- **Eikos: discovery record for training; a possible external comparator later**, and only if we
  score its open weights ourselves on a suite we froze first. Its 4B is the plausible one; the 27B
  is out of scope whatever we do with it.
- **Neither changes an M5 arm.** The ladder stays R1, R2a, R2b, A1, A2a, A2b against R3a.
